# Incident Process

## Incident Flow

1. Detect alert or report.
2. Open incident ticket or channel.
3. Assign incident commander.
4. Triage severity and impact.
5. Mitigate and restore service.
6. Reconstruct timeline.
7. Perform RCA and postmortem.
8. Feed action items into backlog.

## AI Role

AI may summarize alerts, logs, traces, deployment changes, and messages. AI may draft timelines and RCA hypotheses. The incident commander remains responsible for decisions.
